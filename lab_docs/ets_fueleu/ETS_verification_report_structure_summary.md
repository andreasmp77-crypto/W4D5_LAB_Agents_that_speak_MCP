# MRV Maritime EU ETS – Verification Report Structure (Summary)

This document summarises the structure and key elements of the official verification report templates used for MRV Maritime and EU ETS for shipping companies.

It is based on the idea of ship-level emissions reports (ERs), partial emissions reports, and company-level emissions reports (CERs), and the corresponding independent verification opinions.

## Overall Structure of the Verification Report

The verification report templates typically contain the following main components:

- **Opinion Statement**
  - Ship-level ER / partial ER opinion statement.
  - Company-level CER opinion statement.

- **Annex 1 – Findings**
  - Misstatements.
  - Non-conformities.
  - Recommendations for improvement.
  - Prior year non-conformities not resolved.

- **Annex 2 – Basis of Work**
  - Objectives and scope of the verification.
  - Responsibilities of the shipping company, administering authority and verifier.
  - Description of work performed and verification criteria.
  - Reference documents and standards.

This structure supports a clear separation between the core opinion and the detailed background information.

## Opinion Statement – Key Elements

The opinion statement section typically includes:

- **Ship or company details**
  - Name of shipping company.
  - IMO unique company and registered owner identification number.
  - Ship name and IMO ship identification number (for ship-level reports).
  - Administering authority responsible for the monitoring plan and EU ETS scope.

- **Emissions details**
  - Reporting year.
  - Reference document (file name of the emissions report).
  - Date of the emissions report.
  - Total emissions in tonnes of CO₂ equivalent.
  - Fuels used and applied methodologies.
  - Important changes affecting emissions during the reporting year.

- **Site verification details**
  - Whether a site visit was undertaken, waived, or conducted virtually.
  - Dates of visits and person-days on-site.
  - Names of auditors and technical experts involved.
  - Justification for waiving or conducting virtual site visits, where applicable.

- **Compliance checks**
  - Confirmation that monitoring plan requirements were met.
  - Confirmation that MRV Maritime and ETS reporting rules were respected.
  - Summary of whether data verification and control activities were adequate.

- **Opinion types**
  - Verified as satisfactory.
  - Verified as satisfactory with comments.
  - Not verified, with reasons (e.g. material misstatements, non-conformities, or limitations in data or scope).

The opinion text is expressed in positive wording when the report is satisfactory, and a different phrasing is used if the report cannot be verified.

## Annex 1 – Findings (Misstatements, Non-conformities, Improvements)

Annex 1 provides structured lists of:

- **Uncorrected misstatements**
  - Each misstatement is described with nature, size, and affected part of the report.
  - It is indicated whether the misstatement is considered material or non-material.
  - Examples include incorrect totals, misclassified voyages, or wrong emission factors.

- **Uncorrected non-conformities**
  - Each non-conformity describes an instance where legal requirements or procedural rules were not fully met.
  - These may relate to missing monitoring plan approvals, incomplete procedures, or failure to follow required methodologies.

- **Recommendations for improvement**
  - Suggestions for enhancing monitoring and reporting systems.
  - Examples include improved documentation, better data quality checks, or enhanced control activities.

- **Prior year non-conformities not resolved**
  - Outstanding issues carried over from previous years that remain unresolved.
  - Helps track long-term compliance trends and improvement needs.

Annex 1 thus provides detailed context that explains and qualifies the high-level opinion in the main statement.

## Annex 2 – Basis of Work and Reference Documents

Annex 2 outlines the foundation for the verifier’s opinion:

- **Objectives and scope of verification**
  - For ship-level ERs and partial ERs: verifying the ship’s annual emissions and compliance with the monitoring plan and MRV Maritime Regulation.
  - For company-level CERs: verifying the aggregated emissions data and compliance with the EU ETS Directive and MRV Maritime Regulation.

- **Responsibilities**
  - Shipping company: preparation and reporting of emissions data and maintenance of monitoring and control systems.
  - Administering authority: approval of monitoring plans and enforcement of MRV and ETS requirements.
  - Verifier: independent examination and issuance of an opinion to a reasonable level of assurance.

- **Work performed**
  - Description of risk analysis, data verification and analytical procedures.
  - Materiality level and assessment of verification risk.
  - Explanation that GHG quantification involves inherent uncertainty.

- **Reference documents**
  - MRV Maritime Regulation.
  - Delegated regulation on verification and accreditation.
  - Relevant ISO standards on validation and verification of environmental information.
  - Guidance documents prepared by the European Commission and European Cooperation for Accreditation.
  - EU ETS Directive and related guidance.

Understanding this structure helps interpret verification reports and supports building tools or agents that navigate and explain verification outputs.
